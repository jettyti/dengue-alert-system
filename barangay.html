Backup & Recovery
Paper + CSV fallback for power outages or when the system/server is down
🔌
When there's a blackout or the server/system is down
Don't stop recording. Step 1: write cases on the printable paper form below while there's no power or connection. Step 2: once the system is back online, type those paper records into the CSV template and import them all at once — no need to re-encode each case one by one through the form.
📝 Step 1 — Paper Form (offline)
Print copies ahead of time and keep them at the barangay hall
A blank, printable version of the case report — same fields as the online form. Use this by hand when there's no electricity or internet, then encode it later using Step 2.
📥 Step 2 — Bulk Import via CSV
Once power/system is restored
Download the template, fill in one row per paper record (in Excel, Sheets, or Notepad), then upload it here to submit all cases to the RHU in one go.
