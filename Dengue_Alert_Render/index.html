Know Your Enemy
Warning Signs
Watch for these serious symptoms — seek hospital care immediately:
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Persistent vomiting
  • Bleeding gums or nose
  • Rapid breathing or fatigue
  • Blood in urine, stools, or vomit
Classic Symptoms
Dengue fever typically begins 2–7 days after a mosquito bite:
  • Sudden high fever (38–40°C)
  • Severe headache & eye pain
  • Muscle and joint aches
  • Skin rash after 2–5 days
  • Nausea and vomiting

Prevention Tips
Eliminate Breeding Sites
Aedes mosquitoes breed in standing water. Check every week:
  • Empty and scrub water containers
  • Cover drums and water tanks
  • Clear clogged gutters
  • Remove old tires and cans
  • Change pet water daily
Fogging Guidelines
Fogging works best with source reduction. Before fogging:
  • Cover all food and water
  • Remove pets and children
  • Close windows during fogging
  • Stay away for 30 minutes
  • Fogging alone is not enough!
When to Report
Report dengue cases within 24 hours to the RHU:
  • Any confirmed or suspected case
  • Cluster of fever in one area
  • After fogging is completed
  • After a clean-up drive
  • Any outbreak concern
